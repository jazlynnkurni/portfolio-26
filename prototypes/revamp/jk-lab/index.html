The wall, at two letters.

Two hairline cells in Helvetica Neue, the K inked gold with the letter knocked out. It is the hero's own grammar, so it needs no explaining anywhere the hero is.

One stem for both.

The J's stem is the K's stem. The J descends and hooks under; the K's arm and leg strike off the same vertical. Drawn as a stroke, so it holds at 22px and at 150.
