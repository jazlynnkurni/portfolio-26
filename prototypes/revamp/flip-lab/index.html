About · the object

A flip phone that opens when you come near.

Closed, it shows the time in New York. Hover and the lid swings up on its hinge; the screen inside runs the six photographs through a four-tone ink dither. Move over the screen and a soft window follows you and shows the colour underneath; drag to brush the dither away. The keys change the photo.
