Cover lab
Covers

One press for every project.

The same cover through four presses. Each reduces the image to tone and maps the tone onto the palette, so every project reads as one body of work whatever it was shot in. Pick the project above to see how each press handles it; mode flips the ground.

01 · TRITONE

Oxblood, gold, paper.

All three inks: oxblood in the shadows, gold through the middle, paper in the lights, under a 4px screen.

02 · DUOTONE

Oxblood to paper.

Two inks. Quieter and more like a print; the gold is left for the wall and the sheets. Locked: this is what the site runs.

03 · ONE INK, TWO SCREENS

Charcoal, screened twice.

A single ink through two screens at different pitches, so the overlap makes the rosette the riso disc makes. The most "printed" of the four.

04 · GOLD LED

Charcoal, gold, paper, on grain.

The same three as 01 with charcoal in the shadows instead of oxblood, and paper grain instead of a dot screen. Warmer, less graphic.
