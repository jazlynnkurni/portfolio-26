JK, three ways

Each is a real vector (glyphs outlined with fontTools, no font dependency), in charcoal, gold, oxblood and paper. The PNGs in png/ are exported at 1024, 512, 180 (Apple touch icon), 64, 32 and 16. The last row of each shows the mark at the size the nav actually uses, 22px tall, beside the pill.

Sizes to keep in mind. Nav mark: 22px tall. Favicon: 32px and 16px, PNG, plus the SVG for browsers that take it. Apple touch icon: 180px, no transparency, on paper. Open Graph: put the mark at 256px on a 1200x630 paper card. Everything is height-based: set the height, let width follow.

A. The monogram

Bodoni 72 Bold. Hairlines and fat stems, the K tucked under the J. Reads best at 40px and up; at 16px the hairlines go, so the favicon would be the J stem alone.
