JK, from the system

Nothing here is borrowed. Every mark is made of the site's own objects: the hairline cell with its head rule, foot rule and stems; the inked tile with the letter knocked out; and the two faces the site already sets, Helvetica Neue and Plus Jakarta Sans. Charcoal, oxblood, gold, and paper on charcoal, each a true vector.

Sizes to keep in mind. Nav mark 22px tall. Favicon 32 and 16 as PNG plus the SVG. Apple touch icon 180 on paper. Open Graph: the mark at 256 on a 1200x630 paper card. Height sets everything, width follows. PNGs are in png/ at 1024, 512, 180, 64, 32 and 16, transparent.

Two cells of the hero grid, at rest. Helvetica Neue Regular at the site's 34/40 ratio, head rule, foot rule, a stem at each start. This is the site itself as a mark. The hairlines are 1/40 of the height, so below 24px they need the inked variant.
